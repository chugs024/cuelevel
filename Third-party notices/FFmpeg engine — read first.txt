CUELEVEL AUDIO ENGINE (FFMPEG) — WHICH NOTICE APPLIES

CueLevel runs FFmpeg as a separate program inside the app (Contents/Resources/ffmpeg) for decoding, measuring,
filtering and writing audio. It never encodes video; picture streams are copied unchanged.

Release builds (1.0 and later, made with release.command after build-ffmpeg-lgpl.command)
• FFmpeg 7.1 built from the official source release with --disable-gpl --disable-nonfree and no external libraries.
  Licence: GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 or later — see "FFmpeg LGPL-2.1 licence.txt".
• Exact build configuration: Engine/BUILD.txt (also printed by `ffmpeg -buildconf`).
• Corresponding source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.tar.xz (SHA-256 in Engine/ffmpeg-7.1.tar.xz.sha256).
  The same archive is kept with the build in Engine/ so it can be supplied on request or alongside downloads.
• FFmpeg is the work of the FFmpeg developers (https://ffmpeg.org). CueLevel is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Development builds and CueLevel 0.6 and earlier
• Used the imageio-ffmpeg 0.6.0 binary, which enables GPL components. See "FFmpeg build and license.txt",
  "Provenance.txt" and "imageio-ffmpeg LICENSE.txt". Do not distribute that binary commercially without meeting the GPL.

Speech denoiser model: RNNoise (BSD licence) — see "RNNoise LICENSE.txt" and "RNNoise provenance.txt".
