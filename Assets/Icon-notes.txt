CUELEVEL ICON — 2.0 (13 October 2026)
Hand-built vector design: CueLevel-icon.svg is the master; CueLevel-icon.png (1024 px) and CueLevel.icns are exported from it
(rendered with Chromium, packed as PNG entries icp4–ic14, 16–1024 px).

Concept: four uneven bone-coloured bars (a raw cue) become three even green bars (the levelled cue).
Green is the app's "good level" colour. Graphite tile on the macOS icon grid: 824 px body, 185 px corners on a 1024 canvas, no text.
The in-app mark (LogoMark in Theme.swift) draws the same bars.
